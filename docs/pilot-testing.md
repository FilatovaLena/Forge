# Forge pilot — tester guide

Thank you for helping us improve Forge, a place to find co-founders, business partners and people with complementary resources.

**Time:** about 15–20 minutes. You may test on a phone or computer.

**Open:** https://forge-codex-beta.elena-filatova3.chatgpt.site/

Forge is currently private. Ask Elena to grant access to the email used for your ChatGPT account before starting. Sign in with that same account. This is a pilot, so project availability will be limited; listings marked DEMO are examples.

## What to try

1. **Complete your profile.** Add your location, what you can bring to a business and the type of partner or resource you need. Save, refresh and check that your details remain.
2. **Publish a project.** Describe a business idea you are comfortable sharing with the pilot group. Select its category, location, stage and working arrangement. State what help or resources you need. If it is fictional, begin its name with TEST. Check that it appears in Projects after refreshing.
3. **Find an opportunity.** In Discover, use search and the location, category and working-arrangement filters. Open a project that interests you and save it. If there is no suitable project, tell us what was missing rather than inventing a match.
4. **Try a connection.** Coordinate with Elena or another consenting pilot tester. Open a project owned by that person and press Interested. The owner accepts it in Interest; both participants then open Matches and exchange a short test message. Note whether it was clear how an interest becomes a match. You cannot send interest to your own project.
5. **Check your return visit.** Refresh and return to Profile, Saved projects and Matches. Check whether your saved information and conversation are still available. On a phone, check that the keyboard leaves the message field and Send button usable.

Try each task on your own first. If you get stuck, stop at that point and note what you expected to happen. Asking for help is useful feedback, not a failed test.

## Tell us what happened

Copy the [short feedback questionnaire](feedback-form.md), fill it in and send it privately to Elena through your existing contact channel. The questionnaire is a text template, not a web form with an automatic Submit button. Do not post completed responses to this public GitHub repository.

For a problem, include the action, expected result, actual result and device/browser. A screenshot is helpful if it does not reveal anything you do not want to share.

## For Elena — running the pilot

- Start with 5–10 people with a genuine business-partner or resource need. Where possible include both people seeking help and people offering skills or resources.
- Grant access individually. Pair each tester with another consenting tester for the messaging exercise.
- Share this guide and the questionnaire yourself. These documents do not send invitations.
- Count completed tasks out of the people who attempted them; do not count steps blocked by missing access or lack of another tester as product failures.
- Separate technical errors, confusing steps and lack of suitable participants. A working message test does not establish demand for the product.
- After the first five responses, choose the three most frequent or severe obstacles. Fix blockers before inviting more people.
- Keep genuine projects; ask owners to remove their own fictional TEST listings after the pilot. Do not delete other users' records without their authorization.
